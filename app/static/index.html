Method

ResNet-18 (fine-tuned by me). Started from ImageNet weights and fine-tuned on a public real-vs-AI image set, with JPEG compression added during training. It calls an image AI at 0.61 or higher, a cut-off chosen on my validation images.

SwinV2 (frozen baseline). A pretrained detector (haywoodsloan/ai-image-detector-deploy), used as-is. It says "likely AI" only above 0.99 and "no strong AI signal" below 0.10.

Both models run on a cloud GPU. Images are analyzed in memory and never stored.

Limits

  • A probability, not proof. Don't use it as the only basis for accusing anyone.
  • Compression lowers scores. WhatsApp, WeChat and screenshots recompress images. Upload the original file.
  • New generators can evade it. Midjourney v7, FLUX and Firefly 4 are hard for every public detector. A low score does not clear an image.
  • When the models disagree, treat the result as uncertain.
  • JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, GIF, BMP, TIFF · up to 15 MB.
